02   /   TREATMENT SCIENCE

Different tools.
One goal.

RCC treatment depends on the cancer's stage, location, and the patient's overall health. Explore the three major treatment approaches.

✚
01 / LOCAL TREATMENT

Surgery

Doctors remove the tumor, sometimes keeping the rest of the kidney (partial nephrectomy). If necessary, they remove the entire kidney (radical nephrectomy).

OFTEN USED FOR LOCALIZED RCC ↗
⬡
02 / IMMUNE SYSTEM

Immunotherapy

Medicines help the body's immune system recognize and attack cancer cells. Checkpoint inhibitors remove some of the signals that hold immune cells back.

COMMON IN ADVANCED RCC ↗
◎
03 / CELL SIGNALING

Targeted therapy

Drugs interrupt signals cancer cells use to grow. Many RCC treatments reduce signaling that helps tumors develop new blood vessels.

OFTEN COMBINED WITH IMMUNOTHERAPY ↗

Why combine treatments?

Some advanced RCC treatments pair immunotherapy with targeted therapy. Imagine targeted therapy disrupting the cancer's supply lines while immunotherapy helps the immune system attack. Combinations may work better for certain patients, but they can also cause side effects and are not appropriate for everyone.

What researchers are trying to improve

Scientists are developing more effective treatments, studying why some tumors resist treatment, and finding ways to reduce side effects. Immunotherapy can sometimes cause inflammation in healthy organs; targeted therapy may cause high blood pressure, fatigue, or diarrhea. Treatment is always individualized by medical professionals.
